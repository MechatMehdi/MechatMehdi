# Greetings!, I'm Mechat Mehdi

### About Me
I am a Software Engineer and Full-Stack Developer specializing in building production-grade web, mobile, and desktop applications. I hold a degree in Information Systems and Software Engineering (ISIL) from USTHB, combining a rigorous academic foundation with extensive hands-on development experience. At the master's level, I specialized in Software Engineering with a focus on Artificial Intelligence.

As the founder and lead developer of an officially incubated startup, I have practical experience guiding products from initial architectural design to deployment. My technical expertise spans backend engineering with Node.js, Express, PostgreSQL, and Redis for caching and job queues, alongside cross-platform frontend development using React, React Native, and Flutter.

Driven by a passion for efficiency, I actively leverage advanced AI workflows and modern development tools to accelerate development, debug complex codebases, and optimize system performance.
